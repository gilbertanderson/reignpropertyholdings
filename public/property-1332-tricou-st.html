About this property

One side of a duplex in New Orleans' Holy Cross neighborhood, this fully renovated multifamily home blends classic shotgun-style architecture with modern finishes. The open kitchen features stainless steel appliances and custom wood cabinetry, opening onto a comfortable dining and living area.

Outside, a covered front porch and off-street parking for two vehicles complete the home — all just minutes from downtown New Orleans.
