About Reign Property Holdings

A Louisiana limited liability company, woman-owned, focused on rental real estate in the Greater New Orleans area.

Our Mission

Investing in New Orleans housing, one property at a time.

Reign Property Holdings LLC is a Louisiana-based, woman-owned real estate investment company. Our purpose is straightforward: acquire and renovate quality rental housing across the Greater New Orleans area, and see it managed with the kind of hands-on care that comes from working with a local manager rather than a distant call center. Day-to-day management of our homes is handled by StrikeWorks, a veteran-owned company.

We currently own two renovated homes on Tricou Street in New Orleans' Holy Cross neighborhood, managed on our behalf by StrikeWorks, with plans to grow our portfolio while keeping the same standard of quality and responsiveness for every resident.

What We Do
