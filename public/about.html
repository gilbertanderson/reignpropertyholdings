About Reign Property Holdings

A Louisiana limited liability company focused on rental real estate in the Greater New Orleans area.

Our Mission

Investing in New Orleans housing, one property at a time.

Reign Property Holdings LLC is a Louisiana-based real estate investment company. Our purpose is straightforward: acquire, renovate, and manage quality rental housing across the Greater New Orleans area — and do it with the kind of hands-on care that comes from being family-operated rather than run by a distant call center.

We currently own and manage two renovated homes on Tricou Street in New Orleans' Holy Cross neighborhood, with plans to grow our portfolio while keeping the same standard of quality and responsiveness for every resident.

At a glance

  • Louisiana limited liability company
  • Manager-managed structure
  • Family-operated
  • Focused on Greater New Orleans, LA
