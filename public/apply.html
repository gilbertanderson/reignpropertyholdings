Apply Online

Rental applications for every Reign Property Holdings home are handled through TurboTenant — a secure online platform for applications, screening, and rent payments.

How it works

  1. 1
    Pick your home

    Choose the property you'd like to apply for below, or start a general application if you're flexible on which home.

  2. 2
    Complete the TurboTenant application

    You'll be taken to TurboTenant to fill out your rental history, employment, and income. Your information is submitted directly to them — not stored on this website.

  3. 3
    Screening & review

    TurboTenant runs the credit, background, and eviction-history checks, then shares the report with us. Applicants pay the screening fee directly to TurboTenant.

  4. 4
    We follow up

    We review completed applications in the order received and reach out with next steps, usually within a couple of business days.

What you'll need

  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Proof of income (recent pay stubs or offer letter)
  • Two years of residence history with landlord contacts
  • Employer contact information
  • Details for every adult who will live in the home — each applies separately

Already a resident?

Current residents use the TurboTenant resident portal to pay rent online, set up autopay, and submit maintenance requests.
