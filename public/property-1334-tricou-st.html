About this property

One side of a duplex next door to 1332 Tricou St, this fully renovated multifamily home sits in New Orleans' Holy Cross neighborhood. A bright, open-concept living and dining area leads into a renovated kitchen with stainless steel appliances and custom wood cabinetry.

The home includes a comfortable bedroom with hardwood-style flooring and ceiling fans throughout, plus a covered front porch overlooking a well-kept yard.
